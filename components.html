Resistor

A resistor limits the flow of current. Its value in ohms (Ω) is shown by coloured bands. Ohm’s law: V = I × R.

Inductor

A coil of wire that stores energy in a magnetic field. Used in filters, power supplies and radio circuits.

Transformer

Steps AC voltage up or down. This 230 V to 12 V type powers small electronic projects.

Rectifier diode

A diode lets current flow in one direction only. The 1N4007 is used to convert AC to DC.

₹10 for a pack of 10

Zener diode

Works as a voltage regulator. It holds a steady voltage, such as 5.1 V, across a circuit.

₹15 for a pack of 10

LED

A light emitting diode glows when current flows. Always connect a resistor in series.

Transistor (BC547)

An NPN transistor with three legs (base, collector, emitter). Works as a switch or an amplifier.

Crystal oscillator

Produces an accurate clock frequency for microcontrollers and clocks.

Relay module

Integrated circuit (IC)

Thousands of tiny parts packed into one chip. Timers, logic gates and memories all come as ICs.
